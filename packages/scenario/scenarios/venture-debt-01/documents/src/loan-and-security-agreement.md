# Loan and Security Agreement

*Draft of September 22, 2026. Corwin & Pratt LLP draft for discussion. Subject to the Bank's credit approval.*

This Loan and Security Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Closing Date between HALDEN BANK ("Bank") and NORTHLAKE COMPUTE, INC., a Delaware corporation ("Borrower"). The parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 Defined terms

"Annualized Recurring Revenue" means, as of any date, the contracted monthly recurring revenue under executed colocation agreements with a remaining term of at least twelve months, for the most recent fiscal month ended on or before that date, multiplied by twelve, determined in accordance with GAAP.

"Closing Date" means the date this Agreement becomes effective.

"Collateral" has the meaning given in Exhibit A.

"Intellectual Property" means all patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, software and other intellectual property of Borrower, and all applications, registrations and licenses for any of the foregoing.

"Obligations" means all loans, advances, fees, expenses and other amounts owing by Borrower to Bank under the Loan Documents.

"Permitted Liens" means the liens described on Schedule 1.1 of the Disclosure Schedule and the other liens listed in Section 7.2.

"Series C Financing" means the sale by Borrower of its Series C preferred stock for gross cash proceeds of at least $150,000,000.

"Term SOFR" has the meaning given in Schedule 1.2.

"Tranche 1 Availability Period" means the period from the closing of the Series C Financing through the date 30 days thereafter.

"Tranche 2 Availability Period" means the period from the Closing Date through the date 18 months thereafter.

## 2. Loan and Terms of Payment

### 2.1 Tranche 1 advance

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, during the Tranche 1 Availability Period Bank agrees to make one term loan advance to Borrower in the principal amount of $50,000,000 (the "Tranche 1 Advance"). Borrower's right to request the Tranche 1 Advance is conditioned on the closing of the Series C Financing and the satisfaction of the conditions in Section 3.2.

### 2.2 Tranche 2 advance

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, during the Tranche 2 Availability Period Bank agrees to make one term loan advance to Borrower in the principal amount of $50,000,000 (the "Tranche 2 Advance"). Borrower's right to request the Tranche 2 Advance is conditioned on Borrower having achieved Annualized Recurring Revenue of at least $40,000,000 as of the last day of a fiscal quarter, as certified in a Compliance Certificate delivered to Bank, no Event of Default having occurred and being continuing, and the satisfaction of the conditions in Section 3.2.

### 2.3 Interest

(a) Each advance bears interest at a floating rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus 6.50%, provided that Term SOFR shall never be less than 4.00%.

(b) Interest is payable monthly in arrears on the first day of each month.

(c) During the continuance of an Event of Default, at Bank's election, the Obligations bear interest at a rate 3.00% above the rate otherwise applicable.

### 2.4 Repayment

(a) Borrower shall pay interest only on each advance for the 24 months following the Closing Date.

(b) Beginning on the first day of the 25th month after the Closing Date, Borrower shall repay the outstanding principal of each advance in 36 equal monthly installments.

(c) All Obligations are due and payable on the date 60 months after the Closing Date (the "Maturity Date").

### 2.5 Fees

(a) Borrower shall pay a closing fee of $1,000,000 on the Closing Date.

(b) Borrower shall pay a final payment fee equal to 3.50% of the original principal amount of each advance when that advance is repaid in full, whether at maturity, by prepayment or by acceleration.

### 2.6 Prepayment

Borrower may prepay any advance in whole or in part on ten business days' written notice, together with accrued interest, the final payment fee attributable to the amount prepaid and a prepayment premium equal to 3.00% of the principal prepaid if paid during the first year after the Closing Date, 2.00% during the second year and 1.00% thereafter.

### 2.7 Notice of borrowing

Borrower shall give Bank at least ten business days' written notice of a requested advance, in the form of Exhibit B.

## 3. Conditions of Advances

### 3.1 Conditions precedent to closing

The effectiveness of this Agreement is subject to Bank's receipt, in form and substance satisfactory to Bank, of the following:

(a) this Agreement and the other Loan Documents, duly executed;

(b) a certificate of the secretary of Borrower attaching its charter, bylaws, board resolutions and incumbency;

(c) a certificate of good standing of Borrower from the State of Delaware and each state where it is qualified to do business;

(d) the Perfection Certificate, completed and executed by Borrower;

(e) UCC and other lien searches against Borrower, and evidence that all liens other than Permitted Liens have been released or will be released at closing;

(f) evidence of insurance meeting the requirements of Section 6.5, with lender's loss payable and additional insured endorsements in favor of Bank;

(g) a landlord waiver or collateral access agreement, in form satisfactory to Bank, from the landlord of each leased location at which Collateral is located;

(h) a subordination agreement from each holder of Borrower's convertible promissory notes, or evidence that such notes will convert at the Series C Financing;

(i) an intercreditor or subordination agreement with Vantage Equipment Finance on terms satisfactory to Bank;

(j) payment of the closing fee and Bank's legal fees and expenses;

(k) the Warrant, duly executed; and

(l) an opinion of Borrower's counsel.

### 3.2 Conditions precedent to each advance

Bank's obligation to make each advance is subject to the following conditions:

(a) receipt of a notice of borrowing under Section 2.7;

(b) the representations and warranties in Section 5 being true and correct in all material respects on the date of the advance;

(c) no Event of Default or event that with notice or lapse of time would become an Event of Default having occurred and being continuing;

(d) for the Tranche 1 Advance, evidence satisfactory to Bank that the Series C Financing has closed; and

(e) for the Tranche 2 Advance, a Compliance Certificate demonstrating satisfaction of the Annualized Recurring Revenue condition in Section 2.2.

## 4. Creation of Security Interest

### 4.1 Grant of security interest

Borrower hereby grants Bank, to secure the payment and performance in full of all of the Obligations, a continuing security interest in, and pledges to Bank, the Collateral, wherever located, whether now owned or hereafter acquired or arising, and all proceeds and products thereof.

### 4.2 Priority of security interest

Borrower represents, warrants and covenants that the security interest granted herein is and shall at all times continue to be a first priority perfected security interest in the Collateral, subject only to Permitted Liens.

### 4.3 Authorization to file

Borrower authorizes Bank to file financing statements describing the Collateral in any jurisdiction Bank deems appropriate.

## 5. Representations and Warranties

### 5.1 Due organization and authorization

Borrower is duly existing and in good standing in Delaware and is qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where the failure to qualify would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect.

### 5.2 Collateral

Borrower has good title to the Collateral, free of liens other than Permitted Liens. The Collateral is located only at the locations listed in the Perfection Certificate. All of Borrower's deposit accounts are listed in the Perfection Certificate.

### 5.3 Litigation

Except as disclosed on Schedule 5.3, there are no actions or proceedings pending or, to Borrower's knowledge, threatened against Borrower involving more than $500,000.

### 5.4 Financial statements

All financial statements delivered to Bank fairly present in all material respects Borrower's financial condition, and there has been no material adverse change since the date of the most recent statements.

### 5.5 Intellectual Property

Borrower owns or has the right to use all Intellectual Property material to its business, and Schedule 5.5 lists all registered Intellectual Property.

## 6. Affirmative Covenants

### 6.1 Government compliance

Borrower shall maintain its existence and comply with all laws where noncompliance would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect.

### 6.2 Financial statements and reports

(a) Borrower shall deliver monthly financial statements within 30 days after each month end.

(b) Borrower shall deliver annual audited financial statements within 180 days after each fiscal year end.

(c) Borrower shall deliver its board-approved annual budget within 30 days after board approval.

(d) With each delivery under this Section 6.2, Borrower shall deliver a Compliance Certificate in the form of Exhibit C.

### 6.3 Taxes

Borrower shall pay all taxes when due, other than taxes contested in good faith with adequate reserves.

### 6.4 Access

Borrower shall permit Bank to inspect the Collateral and Borrower's books and records on reasonable notice.

### 6.5 Insurance

Borrower shall keep its business and the Collateral insured with financially sound insurers in amounts customary for companies in its industry, with Bank named as lender's loss payee and additional insured.

### 6.6 Accounts

(a) Within 60 days after the Closing Date, Borrower shall cause each institution at which it maintains a deposit account to enter into a control agreement in favor of Bank.

(b) Within 120 days after the Closing Date, Borrower shall maintain all of its operating accounts with Bank.

### 6.7 Landlord waivers

Borrower shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain a landlord waiver or collateral access agreement for any location leased after the Closing Date at which Collateral with a value exceeding $250,000 is located.

### 6.8 Further assurances

Borrower shall execute any further instruments Bank reasonably requests to perfect and continue Bank's lien on the Collateral.

### 6.9 Minimum cash

Borrower shall at all times maintain unrestricted cash in deposit accounts at Bank of not less than $15,000,000, tested as of the last day of each month.

## 7. Negative Covenants

Borrower shall not do any of the following without Bank's prior written consent.

### 7.1 Dispositions

Convey, sell, lease, transfer or otherwise dispose of all or any part of its business or property, other than sales of inventory in the ordinary course, worn-out or obsolete equipment, and non-exclusive licenses of Intellectual Property in the ordinary course of business.

### 7.2 Encumbrance

Create, incur or allow any lien on any of its property, other than Permitted Liens, which include: (a) liens for taxes not yet due; (b) purchase-money liens on equipment securing indebtedness not exceeding $10,000,000 in the aggregate; (c) liens listed on Schedule 1.1; and (d) statutory liens of landlords and contractors arising in the ordinary course for amounts not overdue or being contested in good faith.

### 7.3 Indebtedness

Create or incur any indebtedness other than the Obligations, indebtedness secured by Permitted Liens, and subordinated indebtedness approved by Bank.

### 7.4 Distributions and investments

Pay any dividends or make any distribution on its capital stock, or make any investment, other than as permitted on Schedule 7.4.

### 7.5 Intellectual Property

Create, incur or allow any lien or other encumbrance on any Intellectual Property, or transfer any Intellectual Property other than non-exclusive licenses in the ordinary course of business; and Borrower shall not permit the inclusion in any material contract of any provision that could restrict Bank's ability to exercise remedies against the Collateral.

### 7.6 Change in business

Engage in any business other than the businesses currently engaged in, change its jurisdiction of organization, or change its name without 30 days' prior notice to Bank.

## 8. Events of Default

### 8.1 Payment default

Borrower fails to pay any of the Obligations when due.

### 8.2 Covenant default

Borrower fails to perform any covenant in Section 6.2, 6.6, 6.9 or Section 7, or fails to perform any other covenant and does not cure the failure within 15 days after the earlier of Bank's notice and Borrower's knowledge.

### 8.3 Material adverse change

A material adverse change occurs in Borrower's business, operations or condition, or in the value of the Collateral, or Bank determines in good faith that there is a material impairment in the prospect of repayment of the Obligations.

### 8.4 Attachment; levy

Any material portion of Borrower's assets is attached, seized or levied upon, or a judgment for more than $500,000 is rendered against Borrower and not stayed within 10 days.

### 8.5 Insolvency

Borrower becomes insolvent or commences, or has commenced against it, any insolvency proceeding not dismissed within 45 days.

### 8.6 Other agreements

A default occurs under any agreement with a third party resulting in the acceleration of indebtedness exceeding $500,000.

### 8.7 Misrepresentation

Any representation or warranty made by Borrower to Bank proves to have been false in any material respect when made.

## 9. Bank's Rights and Remedies

### 9.1 Rights and remedies

Upon an Event of Default, Bank may without notice declare all Obligations immediately due and payable, stop advancing money, and exercise all rights and remedies of a secured party under the UCC, including taking possession of and disposing of the Collateral.

### 9.2 Power of attorney

Borrower irrevocably appoints Bank as its attorney-in-fact, effective upon an Event of Default, to endorse Borrower's name on instruments, settle accounts and take any action necessary to realize on the Collateral.

## 10. Notices

Notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set out on the signature pages.

## 11. Choice of Law, Venue and Jury Trial Waiver

New York law governs this Agreement. The parties submit to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New York County and waive trial by jury.

## 12. General Provisions

### 12.1 Successors and assigns

This Agreement binds and benefits the parties' successors and assigns; Borrower may not assign without Bank's consent.

### 12.2 Indemnification

Borrower shall indemnify Bank against all losses arising from this Agreement, other than losses caused by Bank's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 12.3 Entire agreement

The Loan Documents are the entire agreement of the parties and supersede the Summary of Terms dated September 8, 2026.

## Exhibit A – Description of Collateral

The Collateral consists of all of Borrower's right, title and interest in and to all goods, Accounts, Equipment, Inventory, Fixtures, General Intangibles, Deposit Accounts, Investment Property, Commercial Tort Claims, Letter of Credit Rights, Documents, Instruments, Chattel Paper, and all books and records relating to the foregoing, and all proceeds and products of the foregoing.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Collateral does not include any Intellectual Property; provided, however, that the Collateral shall include all Accounts and all proceeds of Intellectual Property, including license royalties and any payments arising from the sale, licensing or other disposition of Intellectual Property.
