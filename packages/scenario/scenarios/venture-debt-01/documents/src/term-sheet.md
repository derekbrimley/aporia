# Summary of Terms

*Venture debt facility, presented by Halden Bank. September 8, 2026. Confidential. Non-binding except as stated in Section 14.*

## 1. Borrower

Northlake Compute, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Borrower"), a venture-backed data center company.

## 2. Lender

Halden Bank (the "Bank").

## 3. Facility

Term loan of up to $100,000,000 (the "Facility"), available in two tranches of $50,000,000 each.

## 4. Tranche 1

$50,000,000, available in a single advance on the closing of the Borrower's Series C preferred stock financing with gross cash proceeds of at least $150,000,000 (the "Series Financing"). The Tranche 1 availability period ends 30 days after the closing of the Series Financing.

## 5. Tranche 2

$50,000,000, available in a single advance when the Borrower achieves revenue of [●], provided no default has occurred. The Tranche 2 availability period ends 18 months after closing.

## 6. Collateral

A first-priority lien on substantially all assets of the Borrower, other than intellectual property. Deposit account control agreements over all deposit accounts within 90 days after closing.

## 7. Interest rate

Term SOFR plus 6.50% per annum, with a Term SOFR floor of 4.00%. Interest is payable monthly in arrears. During the continuance of an event of default the rate increases by 3.00%.

## 8. Repayment

Interest only for 24 months from closing, followed by 36 equal monthly payments of principal plus interest. All amounts are due on the maturity date, 60 months after closing.

## 9. Fees

Closing fee of 1.00% of the Facility, payable at closing. Final payment fee of 3.50% of the principal amount of each advance, payable when that advance is repaid or on maturity.

## 10. Prepayment

Prepayment is permitted in whole or in part on ten business days' notice, with a prepayment premium of 3.00% of the principal prepaid in year one, 2.00% in year two and 1.00% thereafter.

## 11. Warrants

Warrant coverage of 1.50% of the Facility, exercisable for shares of the Borrower's Series C preferred stock.

## 12. Covenants and reporting

Customary affirmative and negative covenants for facilities of this type, including customary financial covenants to be agreed. Monthly financial statements within 30 days of month end, annual audited statements within 180 days of year end, and an annual board-approved budget.

## 13. Conditions

Customary conditions to closing, including satisfactory completion of the Bank's legal and financial diligence, executed loan documents, evidence of perfection of the Bank's liens and no material adverse change.

## 14. Exclusivity and expenses

Upon countersignature the Borrower agrees not to solicit or accept alternative debt financing proposals for 30 days. The Borrower will reimburse the Bank's reasonable legal fees and expenses whether or not the Facility closes. Sections 14 and 15 are binding.

## 15. Governing law

New York.
