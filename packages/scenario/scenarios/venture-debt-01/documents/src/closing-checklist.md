# Closing Checklist

*Halden Bank / Northlake Compute, Inc. $100,000,000 venture debt facility. Corwin & Pratt LLP draft. Target closing: November 20, 2026.*

## 1. Loan documents

(a) Loan and Security Agreement. Responsible: C&P. Status: draft circulated.

(b) Disclosure Schedule. Responsible: Borrower / WM. Status: open.

(c) Perfection Certificate. Responsible: Borrower / WM. Status: open.

(d) Warrant to purchase Series C preferred stock. Responsible: C&P. Status: to be circulated.

(e) Notice of Borrowing (Tranche 1). Responsible: Borrower. Status: at funding.

## 2. Corporate deliverables

(a) Secretary's certificate with charter, bylaws, resolutions and incumbency. Responsible: WM. Status: open.

(b) Good standing certificates: Delaware, Colorado, Nevada. Responsible: WM. Status: ordered.

(c) Opinion of Borrower's counsel. Responsible: WM. Status: open.

## 3. Perfection and lien matters

(a) UCC-1 financing statement, Delaware Secretary of State. Responsible: C&P. Status: to be filed at closing.

(b) UCC, tax and judgment lien searches. Responsible: C&P. Status: ordered.

(c) Lien releases or subordination for liens not on Schedule 1.1. Responsible: Borrower / WM. Status: open.

(d) Landlord waiver: Aurora, Colorado (Ridgeline Industrial Partners LLC). Responsible: Borrower / WM. Status: open.

(e) Landlord waiver: Reno, Nevada (Truckee Meadows Realty Co.). Responsible: Borrower / WM. Status: open.

(f) Intercreditor or subordination agreement with Vantage Equipment Finance. Responsible: C&P / WM. Status: open.

(g) Subordination agreements from holders of convertible notes, or evidence of conversion. Responsible: WM. Status: open.

(h) Deposit account control agreements (post-closing, within 60 days). Responsible: Borrower / WM. Status: post-closing.

## 4. Insurance

(a) Certificates of insurance with lender's loss payable and additional insured endorsements. Responsible: Borrower. Status: open.

## 5. Fees and funding

(a) Closing fee ($1,000,000) and Bank's legal fees. Responsible: Borrower. Status: at closing.

(b) Evidence of Series C Financing closing (condition to Tranche 1 funding). Responsible: Borrower / WM. Status: pending Series C.
