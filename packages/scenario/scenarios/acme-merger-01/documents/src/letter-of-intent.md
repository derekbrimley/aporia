# Letter of Intent

*Signed {{dates.loi}}. Non-binding except as stated in Section 8.*

**Beta Pharma, Inc.** to the Board of Directors of **Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc.** and **Larchmont Healthcare Partners III, L.P.**

This letter sets out the principal terms on which Beta Pharma, Inc. ("Beta") proposes to acquire Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("Acme").

## 1. Transaction structure

A newly formed, wholly owned subsidiary of Beta ("Merger Sub") will merge with and into Acme, with Acme surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Beta (the "Merger"). Beta intends that Acme's corporate existence, contracts, licenses, permits, abbreviated new drug applications and regulatory relationships remain in place. Beta will not assume any liabilities of Acme.

## 2. Consideration

Acme is insolvent and its equity has no value. At the effective time of the Merger every outstanding share of Acme common stock and preferred stock, and every option, warrant or other right to acquire Acme stock, will be cancelled for no consideration.

## 3. Arrangements with BigBank

The Merger is conditioned on BigBank, N.A. ("BigBank") entering into the arrangements described in BigBank's Restructuring Term Sheet delivered with this letter, including forgiveness of $40,000,000 of Acme's obligations, the release of liens to the extent necessary to permit the Merger, and an amended and restated credit agreement for the remaining $40,000,000.

## 4. Conditions

Closing will be subject to:

(a) execution of a definitive merger agreement satisfactory to Beta;

(b) Acme stockholder approval;

(c) the BigBank arrangements described in Section 3;

(d) receipt of material third-party consents; and

(e) other conditions customary for a transaction of this kind.

## 5. Due diligence

Acme will give Beta and its advisers full access to its books, records, contracts, facilities, personnel and regulatory files. Beta's obligations are subject to satisfactory completion of its due diligence.

## 6. Definitive agreement

The definitive agreement will contain representations, warranties and covenants customary for a transaction of this kind. Indemnification to be agreed.

## 7. Exclusivity

For 30 days after the date of this letter Acme will not solicit, encourage or negotiate any other proposal to acquire Acme or a material part of its assets.

## 8. Effect of this letter

Except for Section 7 (Exclusivity) and this Section 8, which are binding, this letter is a non-binding expression of intent. No party is bound to complete the transaction unless and until a definitive agreement is signed.

Accepted and agreed: Beta Pharma, Inc.; Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc.; Larchmont Healthcare Partners III, L.P.
