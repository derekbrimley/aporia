# Pending Litigation Summary

*Prepared by Garrow & Finch LLP for the data room as of {{dates.data_room_open}}. Privileged material omitted.*

## 1. Product liability

### 1.1 Hartwell v. Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Filed in the Superior Court of Wake County, North Carolina, by a patient who received a dose from the recalled lot. Claims negligence and strict liability. Damages claimed of about $3,500,000.

### 1.2 Estate of Morales v. Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Filed in the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina by the estate of a patient who received a dose from the recalled lot. Claims wrongful death and negligence. Damages claimed of about $2,500,000.

## 2. Insurance

Acme's product liability insurer has acknowledged notice of both claims and has reserved its rights, citing the policy's exclusion for known manufacturing deficiencies.

## 3. Other matters

No other litigation is pending or, to Acme's knowledge, threatened, other than routine collection matters brought by trade creditors.
