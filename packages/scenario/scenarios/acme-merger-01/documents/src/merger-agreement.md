# Agreement and Plan of Merger

*Garrow & Finch LLP comments of {{dates.data_room_open}} on Thornbury Vale LLP draft. Abridged: only the sections relevant to the open points are reproduced. Acme's changes are described in bracketed drafting notes.*

This Agreement and Plan of Merger is entered into among Beta Pharma, Inc. ("Parent"), Beta Merger Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub") and Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the "Company").

## 1. The Merger

### 1.1 The Merger

On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement and in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law (the "DGCL"), at the Effective Time Merger Sub shall be merged with and into the Company, the separate corporate existence of Merger Sub shall cease, and the Company shall continue as the surviving corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (the "Surviving Corporation").

### 1.2 Effective Time

The Merger shall become effective when the certificate of merger is filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, or at a later time specified in it (the "Effective Time").

### 1.3 Effect of the Merger

At the Effective Time the Surviving Corporation shall possess all the property, rights, privileges and powers, and be subject to all the debts, liabilities and duties, of the Company and Merger Sub, as provided in Section 259 of the DGCL.

## 2. Effect on capital stock

### 2.1 Cancellation of Company stock

At the Effective Time each share of Company capital stock issued and outstanding immediately before the Effective Time shall be cancelled and shall cease to exist, and no consideration shall be delivered in exchange for it.

### 2.2 Merger Sub stock

Each share of Merger Sub common stock shall be converted into one share of common stock of the Surviving Corporation.

### 2.3 Dissenting shares

Shares held by a holder who has not consented to the Merger and has properly demanded appraisal under Section 262 of the DGCL shall not be cancelled under Section 2.1 but shall entitle the holder only to the rights granted by Section 262.

### 2.4 Options

All Company stock options shall be cancelled at the Effective Time for no consideration.
