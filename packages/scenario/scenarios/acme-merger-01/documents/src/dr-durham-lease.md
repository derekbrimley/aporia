# Lease: Durham Manufacturing Facility

*Between Triangle Research Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("Tenant"). Excerpts posted by Garrow & Finch LLP.*

## 1. Premises

### 1.1 Premises

Landlord leases to Tenant the manufacturing and laboratory building and associated land in Durham, North Carolina described in Exhibit A (the "Premises"), which Tenant has improved as an FDA-registered sterile manufacturing facility.

## 3. Term

### 3.1 Term

The term ends on the fifteenth anniversary of the commencement date, with two five-year renewal options.

## 15. Assignment and subletting

### 15.1 Consent required

Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

### 15.2 Deemed assignment

Any transfer, by merger or otherwise, of more than 50% of the voting stock of Tenant is deemed an assignment of this Lease requiring Landlord's consent under Section 15.1, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

### 15.3 Consent fee

Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable legal fees in reviewing any request for consent.
