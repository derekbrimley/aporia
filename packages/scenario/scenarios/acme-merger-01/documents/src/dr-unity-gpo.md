## 2. Award

### 2.1 Awarded products

Unity awards Vendor a sole-source agreement for the products listed in Exhibit B for Unity's member hospitals. Purchases by Unity members represent the largest share of Vendor's remaining sales.

## 9. Term and termination

### 9.1 Term

This Agreement has a term of three years from its effective date.

### 9.2 Termination for convenience

Unity may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' notice.

### 9.3 Supply failure

Unity may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if Vendor fails to fill at least 95% of member orders for any awarded product for two consecutive months.

## 11. Assignment

### 11.1 No assignment without consent

Vendor shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether by merger, operation of law, change of control or otherwise, without Unity's prior written consent. Any purported assignment without that consent is void, and Unity may terminate this Agreement immediately on notice.

### 11.2 Notice of change of control

Vendor shall notify Unity in writing at least 30 days before any change of control of Vendor.
