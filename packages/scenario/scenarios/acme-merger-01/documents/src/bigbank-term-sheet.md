# BigBank Restructuring Term Sheet

*Delivered with the Letter of Intent of {{dates.loi}}. Indicative and non-binding, subject to BigBank's credit approval and definitive documentation.*

**Borrower:** Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("Acme"). **Lender:** BigBank, N.A. ("BigBank"). **Guarantor:** Beta Pharma, Inc. ("Beta").

## 1. Existing obligations

Term loans of $80,000,000 are outstanding under the Credit Agreement between Acme and BigBank, secured by a first-priority lien on substantially all of Acme's assets. Acme is in default and BigBank is forbearing under the Forbearance Agreement.

## 2. Forgiveness

At the effective time of the Merger BigBank will forgive $40,000,000 of the outstanding term loans (the "Forgiven Amount"), and accrued and unpaid interest and default interest on the Forgiven Amount.

## 3. Guaranty

Beta will guarantee the remaining $40,000,000 and all other obligations under the Amended and Restated Credit Agreement. The guaranty will be a guaranty of payment, not of collection, and will be unsecured.

## 4. Amended and Restated Credit Agreement

The remaining $40,000,000 will remain outstanding under an amended and restated credit agreement: maturity five years after closing; interest at Term SOFR plus 4.25% per annum; financial covenants tested at the Beta level; existing events of default waived at closing.

## 5. Liens

BigBank will release its liens to the extent necessary to permit the Merger, and will keep a first-priority lien on Acme's assets securing the remaining $40,000,000.

## 6. Forbearance and timing

BigBank will continue to forbear under the Forbearance Agreement until {{dates.forbearance_expiry}}, but the forbearance will terminate early if a definitive merger agreement among Beta, Merger Sub and Acme is not signed by {{dates.target_signing}}. On termination of the forbearance BigBank may exercise all of its rights and remedies.

## 7. Conditions

BigBank's obligations are conditioned on: (a) the Merger closing simultaneously; (b) Beta's guaranty and the Amended and Restated Credit Agreement being executed and delivered; (c) no material adverse change in Acme since the date of this term sheet, other than as disclosed to BigBank; and (d) satisfactory documentation.
