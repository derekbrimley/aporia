# Supply Agreement

*Between Halvorsen Chemie GmbH ("Supplier") and Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("Purchaser"). Excerpts posted by Garrow & Finch LLP.*

## 2. Supply

### 2.1 Products

Supplier shall manufacture and supply to Purchaser the active pharmaceutical ingredients listed in Exhibit A (the "API"). The API is used in Purchaser's three highest-revenue products, and Supplier is the only source qualified in Purchaser's ANDAs for those products.

### 2.4 Qualification of alternate sources

Purchaser may qualify an alternate source of API at its own cost. Purchaser acknowledges that qualification of an alternate source requires FDA approval of a supplement to each affected ANDA.

## 13. Term

### 13.1 Term

This Agreement continues until the seventh anniversary of its effective date and renews automatically for successive two-year periods unless either party gives 12 months' notice.

## 14. Termination

### 14.1 Termination for breach

Either party may terminate this Agreement on 60 days' notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure within that period.

### 14.2 Change of control

Supplier may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice to Purchaser at any time within 180 days after a Change of Control of Purchaser. "Change of Control" means any transaction or series of transactions by which any person or group acquires, directly or indirectly, more than 50% of the voting power of Purchaser, including by merger.

### 14.3 Effect of termination

On termination Supplier shall fill purchase orders accepted before the termination date and shall have no further supply obligation.

## 18. Assignment

### 18.1 Assignment

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
