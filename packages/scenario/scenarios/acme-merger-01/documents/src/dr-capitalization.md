# Capitalization Summary and Charter Excerpts

*Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. Prepared by management as of {{dates.data_room_open}}. Unaudited.*

## 1. Outstanding equity

| Class | Holders | Share of total voting power |
|---|---|---|
| Common stock | Founders, current and former employees (about 40 holders of record) | 13% |
| Series A preferred stock | Larchmont Healthcare Partners III, L.P. | 39% |
| Series B preferred stock | Larchmont (48% of the class), Ostrander Ventures (31%), Pinecrest Capital (21%) | 48% |

Larchmont holds 62% of the total voting power, voting together as a single class.

## 2. Liquidation preference

The Series A and Series B preferred stock carry a combined liquidation preference of $95,000,000, senior to the common stock.

## 3. Options

About 120 employees hold options under the 2019 Stock Incentive Plan. All options are out of the money. Section 11 of the plan permits the board to cancel outstanding options for no consideration in a merger.

## 4. Charter excerpt: Series B protective provisions

Article IV, Section 6(c). So long as any shares of Series B Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Corporation shall not, without the written consent or affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of the then outstanding shares of Series B Preferred Stock, voting as a separate class, (i) effect any merger or consolidation of the Corporation with or into any other entity, or (ii) amend this Certificate of Incorporation in a manner adverse to the Series B Preferred Stock.

## 5. Charter excerpt: action by written consent

Article VIII. Any action required or permitted to be taken by the stockholders may be taken without a meeting by written consent signed by the holders of outstanding stock having not less than the minimum number of votes necessary to take the action at a meeting.
