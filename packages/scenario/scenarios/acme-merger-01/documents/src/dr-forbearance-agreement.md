# Forbearance Agreement

*Between Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("Borrower") and BigBank, N.A. ("Lender"). Executed copy, excerpts.*

## 1. Acknowledgments

1.1 Borrower acknowledges that term loans of $80,000,000 are outstanding under the Credit Agreement, that the Specified Defaults (breach of the minimum EBITDA and fixed charge coverage covenants) have occurred and are continuing, and that Lender's liens are valid, perfected and first-priority.

## 2. Forbearance

2.1 Lender agrees to forbear from exercising its rights and remedies in respect of the Specified Defaults until the earliest of (a) {{dates.forbearance_expiry}}, (b) any new Event of Default, and (c) Borrower's failure to satisfy any Sale Milestone (the "Forbearance Termination Date").

2.2 Sale Milestones. Borrower shall (a) pursue a sale transaction with Beta Pharma, Inc. on terms acceptable to Lender, and (b) cause a definitive agreement for that transaction to be signed no later than {{dates.target_signing}}.

## 3. Cash management

3.1 Borrower shall deliver a 13-week cash flow forecast to Lender every week and shall not deviate from it by more than 15% on any line without Lender's consent.

## 4. Release

4.1 Borrower releases Lender from all claims arising before the date of this Agreement.
