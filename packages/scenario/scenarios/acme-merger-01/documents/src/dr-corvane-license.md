# Patent License Agreement

*Between Corvane Biologics, Inc. ("Licensor") and Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("Licensee"). Excerpts posted by Garrow & Finch LLP.*

## 2. License grant

### 2.1 Grant

Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive license under the Licensed Patents to make, use and sell the two Licensed Products identified in Schedule 1, each of which uses Licensor's patented stabilizing formulation.

## 4. Royalties

### 4.1 Royalty

Licensee shall pay Licensor a royalty of 6% of net sales of Licensed Products.

## 9. Term and termination

### 9.1 Term

This Agreement continues until the expiration of the last Licensed Patent.

### 9.2 Termination for breach

Licensor may terminate this Agreement on 60 days' notice for an uncured material breach.

### 9.3 Change of control in favor of a competitor

Licensor may terminate this Agreement on 90 days' notice if Licensee undergoes a change of control (including by merger) as a result of which Licensee becomes controlled by a Competitor. "Competitor" means any person that, directly or through an affiliate, sells a product that competes with a product of Licensor.

## 12. Assignment

### 12.1 Assignment

Licensee may not assign this Agreement without Licensor's prior written consent.
