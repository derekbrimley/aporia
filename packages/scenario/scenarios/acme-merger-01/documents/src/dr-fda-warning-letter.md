# FDA Warning Letter

*U.S. Food and Drug Administration, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research. Issued {{dates.warning_letter}}. Recipient: Acme Pharmaceuticals, Inc., Durham, North Carolina. Fictional letter for this matter.*

## 1. Summary

The FDA inspected Acme's Durham drug manufacturing facility and identified significant violations of current good manufacturing practice regulations for finished pharmaceuticals. Because of these violations, drugs manufactured at the facility are adulterated within the meaning of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.

## 2. Violations

### 2.1 Sterility assurance

Acme failed to establish and follow appropriate written procedures designed to prevent microbiological contamination of drug products purporting to be sterile, including validation of aseptic processes. A lot of one oncology injectable was later recalled.

### 2.2 Data integrity

Acme failed to exercise appropriate controls over computer systems to assure that only authorized personnel make changes to laboratory records, and failed to retain complete laboratory data.

## 3. Required response

Acme must respond in writing within 15 working days with a description of the specific steps taken to correct the violations and prevent recurrence, and a timeline for completion.

## 4. Consequences

Until the violations are corrected the FDA may withhold approval of any new applications or supplements listing the Durham facility, and may take further action without notice, including seizure and injunction.

## 5. Status (added by Acme management)

Acme responded within the deadline and committed to a remediation plan with an estimated cost of $9,000,000. Remediation is in progress and not complete. The FDA has not closed out the warning letter. Acme's subsequent correspondence with the FDA has not been posted.
